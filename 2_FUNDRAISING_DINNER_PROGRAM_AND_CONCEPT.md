# CAMPAIGN LAUNCH & FUNDRAISING DINNER
## Master Concept Note, Program & Execution Guide
### Candidate: Nancy Wamaitha Njenga | MCA Wanjohi Ward (Kipipiri, Nyandarua)

---

## 1. Event Overview

- **Event Theme:** *"An Evening of Resolve: Fueling the Journey of Wamaitha for Wanjohi Ward"*
- **Primary Objectives:**
  1. Unveil Nancy Wamaitha Njenga's vision, candidacy, and political brand.
  2. Raise starting campaign war-chest capital (Logistics, branding merchandise, community ground engagement, nomination fees).
  3. Form a dedicated network of Patrons, Ward Ambassadors, and Resource Mobilizers.
- **Atmosphere:** Dignified, inspiring, high-energy, well-organized, and politically electric.

## 4. Key Campaign Team Roles on Dinner Night

1. **The Master of Ceremony (MC):**
   - Must be politically sharp, fluent in English, Swahili, and conversational Kikuyu.
   - Skilled at warm fundraising facilitation without making guests feel pressured.
2. **Finance & Treasury Desk (2 Persons):**
   - Stationed with a secure cash box, official receipt books, M-Pesa transaction log, and laptop spreadsheet to tally in real time.
3. **Protocol & Ushering (4 Persons):**
   - Ensure guests are guided to their assigned tables, pledge envelopes are distributed at the tables, and collected discreetly.
4. **Media & Communications:**
   - High-definition photography and videography for campaign banners, social media clips, and future campaign profile leaflets.
