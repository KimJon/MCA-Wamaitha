The Wanjohi Manifesto

Five Grassroots Pillars
for Wanjohi Ward

Grounded in the cold mist of the Aberdares, tackling the daily pain of potato and dairy farmers.

🥔 1. Kilimo na Bei Bora

Strict enforcement of the 50kg standardized potato bag rule; Ward potato aggregation hubs & cold storage.

🚜 2. All-Weather Feeder Roads

Aggressive County Assembly budget fighting for graveling and culverts on impassable farm-to-market feeder roads.

🎓 3. Fair & Transparent Bursaries

100% public, automated vetting ensuring every single shilling reaches the child of the peasant and the widow.

🏥 4. Afya Bora & Clean Water

Consistently stocked local dispensaries with medicines and gravity-piped clean water from Aberdare sources.

🏍️ 5. Women & Youth Inclusion

County revolving funds for chamas; lighted Boda Boda customer sheds; local youth access to county tenders.

⚖️ 6. Assemblies Oversight

Fearless defense of Wanjohi's rightful share in Ol Kalou. No more silent MCAs!

Polling Day Protection

The 70-Agent Defense Grid

Elections are won at the polling stream and defended at the tally center.

👥 The 2-Agent Rule per Stream

Every single one of the 35 polling streams in Wanjohi Ward will have 2 vetted agents:
1. Chief Polling Agent: Inside the room observing ballot issuance, stamping, counting, and signing Form 36A.
2. Mobilizing Agent: Outside the gate cross-referencing turnout lists and mobilizing supporters.

📱 Digital Rapid Results Tally

• Immediate photo transmission of Form 36A/34A from each stream to the Front Office command desk.
• Parallel tallying system completed within 2 hours of polls closing.
• Zero tolerance for vote tampering or unvouched results.
