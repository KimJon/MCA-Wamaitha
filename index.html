The Unvarnished Ground Truth

Why Wanjohi Is Weeping: Four Urgent Crises

Behind the political speeches lies the painful daily struggle of our households. Nancy Wamaitha’s resolve is forged in these four realities:

1. Abandoned Youth in Quarries & Drugs

Wanjohi polytechnics have no teachers, no tools, and no budget. Desperate youth are left breaking their backs in stone quarries breathing dust, or drifting into depression and illicit brew.

2. Boda Boda Dying Under Fuel Hikes

Riders brave freezing Aberdare rain, only to have their daily earnings wiped out by soaring fuel prices and broken roads. Politicians use them as rally flags and dump them.

3. Dairy Heartbreak: ZERO Milk Coolers

Wanjohi is a top milk production belt, yet there is not a single milk cooling plant (coolant) in the ward. When rains block roads, our mothers' milk goes sour at the gate while brokers loot them.

4. Empty Dispensaries & Muddy Roads

Mothers carry feverish babies on motorbikes through knee-deep mud, only to be told: "Hakuna dawa." County feeder road funds disappear while farm produce rots in the shamba.
