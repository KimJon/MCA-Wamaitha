The People's Charter

Ending the Raw Deal: Five Grassroots Pillars

Nyandarua feeds Nairobi, yet Wanjohi's farmers remain financially strained. It is time to end this injustice with sharp, aggressive County Assembly oversight.

1. Stop the Potato Exploitation

Strict enforcement of the 50kg standard bag. Establishment of ward-level potato aggregation stores and cold holding rooms to kill the broker cartel.

2. Farm-to-Market Feeder Roads

Fighting on the floor of the County Assembly for heavy graveling, culverts, and bridges on feeder roads so milk and potatoes don't rot in the mud.

3. Transparent, Public Bursaries

Ending the shameful practice where bursaries are handed out as political favors. A published, community-vetted list where every needy child gets funds.

4. Stocked Dispensaries & Clean Water

Ending the excuse of "no medicine" in our local health centers, and tapping gravity water piping directly from Aberdare catchment sources.

5. Dignity for Boda Boda & Youth

Shaded, well-lit Boda Boda customer sheds, safety training, and reserving county maintenance contracts for local youth and women chamas.
