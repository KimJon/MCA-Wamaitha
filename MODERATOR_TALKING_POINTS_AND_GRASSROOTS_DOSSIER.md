# MODERATOR'S MASTER DOSSIER & GRASSROOTS TALKING POINTS
## Campaign of Nancy Wamaitha Njenga | MCA Wanjohi Ward (Kipipiri, Nyandarua)
### Theme: "Daughter of the Soil, Patient in Service, Shrewd in Battle"

---

## Part 1: Character Framing for the Moderator

As the Moderator at the dinner and grassroots town halls, your job is to kill any impression of a generic, detached politician. You must present Nancy Wamaitha as an authentic force of nature rooted in Wanjohi.

Use these three core identity anchors whenever you speak:

### 1. "Daughter of the Soil" (*Mũirĩtu wa Tiri*)
* **Moderator Soundbite:**  
  *"Look at Nancy Wamaitha. She is not an imported guest in Wanjohi. Her feet know the red clay of this land. When she talks about the Aberdare cold biting the fingers of a mother digging potatoes at 5:00 AM, she is not reciting a textbook—she has lived it. She is bone of our bone and flesh of our soil."*

### 2. "Patient & Enduring" (*Mũkirĩrĩria*)
* **Moderator Soundbite:**  
  *"In Kikuyu, we have a sacred saying: 'Mũkirĩrĩria ndatũraga arĩ thĩ'—the one who is patient will never be left on the ground. Nancy did not wake up yesterday and say 'I want to be a politician.' She has sat in our church pews, walked with women in our chamas, stood by families in times of grief, and listened quietly while others chased noise. Her patience is now our strength."*

### 3. "Shrewd, Battle-Tested & Vigilant" (*Njũhĩga*)
* **Moderator Soundbite:**  
  *"Let's speak the raw truth in this room: the people of Wanjohi have been screwed over for too long! Potato brokers screw our mothers at the weighing scale with extended sacks (bag ya rura). Politicians screw our youth with empty promises and disappear to Nairobi. County cartels divert our feeder road gravel.  
  We don't need a soft, naive MCA who will go to Ol Kalou to be sweet-talked with tea and mandazi! We need a shrewd, battle-tested woman who knows the game, detects every trick, and has the courage to say: 'Touch Wanjohi's money and you will face Wamaitha!' That is why she carries that mighty name!"*

## Part 3: Climax Introduction Before Calling Nancy Wamaitha to Speak

*(Lower your voice, create a hush in the room, speak with deep reverence, and build the tempo)*:

> *"Distinguished guests, patrons, elders, and leaders of Kipipiri:  
> Look around this room. We did not come here tonight to crown another cosmetic politician. We came here to stand with one of our own.  
> A woman who was shaped by the red clay of Wanjohi.  
> A woman who has walked with our mothers through the cold seasons without complaining.  
> A patient daughter who listened before she spoke.  
> A shrewd, fearless defender who will never allow the people of Wanjohi to be taken for a ride ever again.  
> Please be upstanding, put your hands together, and welcome with thunderous applause, the incoming Member of County Assembly for Wanjohi Ward—**MAMA NANCY WAMAITHA NJENGA!**"*

---

## Part 4: The Fundraising Transition (Bridging Emotion to Cash)

*(Deliver this immediately after Nancy's address)*:

> *"Asante sana, Mheshimiwa Wamaitha! That is the voice of a true daughter of the soil!  
> Now, ladies and gentlemen, let us be very practical. A campaign is not won by applause in a dining hall. A campaign is won when the candidate has the logistical fuel to reach Kigogo, to reach Huhirio, to reach Satima, and to defend every vote in all 35 polling streams.  
> Nancy Wamaitha has given us her heart, her courage, and her name. She has laid down her life for Wanjohi. Now it is our turn to put the ammunition in her hands.  
> We have pledge cards on every table. We have our Paybill on the screen.  
> Let our Platinum Patrons and Kipipiri leaders show the way! Stand with Wamaitha tonight!"*
