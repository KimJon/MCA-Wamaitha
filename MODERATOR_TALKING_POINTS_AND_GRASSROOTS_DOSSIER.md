### 2. The Archetype: "MJASIRI" (The Fearless Warrior)

When county resources are being shared, Wamaitha will not sit quietly at the back of the room sipping tea. She has the courage of her ancestors to demand every single coin that belongs to Wanjohi Ward!"*

## Part 2: The Ground Crises to Emphasize in the Room

Speak directly to the conscience of the professionals, business people, and diaspora in the room:

1. **Abandoned Youth, Broken Polytechnics & Quarries:**
   * *"Do you know what hurts most in Wanjohi today? Our polytechnics are dead shells—no teachers, no tools, no budget! Our bright young sons are breaking their backs in **stone quarries** breathing dust for KES 200 a day, or sinking into depression and cheap liquor. Nancy Wamaitha says: our children are meant for greatness, not stone quarries!"*
2. **The Dying Boda Boda Trade & Relentless Fuel Hikes:**
   * *"Our young boda boda operators are working from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM, but with the brutal price of fuel and bad roads, they take home nothing. Politicians only remember them during rallies to shout slogans. Wamaitha will build sheltered, lighted customer stages and fight for county SACCO support!"*
3. **The Dairy Heartbreak: ZERO Milk Coolers in Wanjohi:**
   * *"How can Wanjohi be one of the top milk-producing wards in Nyandarua, yet **we do not have a single milk cooling plant (coolant)?** When the rains hit and roads turn into clay soup, our mothers' milk goes sour at the gate! Middlemen rob our farmers. Wamaitha will make milk cooling plants in Upper and Lower Wanjohi non-negotiable in the county budget!"*
4. **Understocked Dispensaries & Feeder Roads:**
   * *"Why must our mothers walk miles to buy basic painkillers because local dispensaries are empty? Wamaitha will partner with the Governor for proper legislation, but she will keep an eagle-eyed **Accountability Check** on every shilling meant for Wanjohi’s health centers and gravel roads!"*
